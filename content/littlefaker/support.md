### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I give a menu item a keyboard shortcut?

Open Settings from the Little Faker menu. Under Keyboard Shortcuts, choose the item from Add Shortcut…, click its field and press the keys. A shortcut needs ⌘ or ⌃, and works in every app. There are shortcuts for Open Menu and Copy Again too.

If another app already uses the keys, Settings says so and doesn't save them.

#### How do I change the language?

Pick it in the Language menu in Little Faker's menu, or in Settings. It sets what Words, Sentences and Paragraphs copy, and which country Fake Info looks like. It starts on your Mac's language.

The menu and Settings themselves follow your Mac's language.

#### What do the tips unlock?

Nothing. Every feature is free. The three tips in Settings are only a way to support the app if you'd like to.

#### Can I use Little Faker in Shortcuts or Spotlight?

Yes. Get Fake Text copies words, sentences or paragraphs in any language. Find it in the Shortcuts app, or in Spotlight on macOS 26.

#### Which macOS does Little Faker need?

Version 1.2 needs macOS 15 or later. On macOS 12 to 14 the Mac App Store offers 1.1.2, the last version that runs there.

#### Settings opens every time I restart my Mac

Fixed in version 1.1.2. Update Little Faker from the Mac App Store.

#### macOS showed "Login Item Added" when I turned on Open at login

That's normal. macOS shows it once for any app that adds a login item. You can manage login items in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.
