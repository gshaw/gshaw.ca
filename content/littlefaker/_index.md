title: Little Faker
---

Fast fake text generator for macOS. Free. No ads. Privacy respected.

Little Faker sits in your menu bar and copies placeholder text and fake data to the clipboard. Pick an item, then paste it anywhere.

<img alt="Screenshot of Little Faker" src="screenshot.png" style="max-width: 100%">

## Features

- Lorem ipsum, or text that reads like real writing in English, German, French, Japanese or Simplified Chinese. Pick the language in the menu.
- Fake Info for eight countries: names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, usernames, job titles and dates of birth that look American, Canadian, British, Australian, German, French, Japanese or Chinese.
- Phone numbers come from the ranges a country keeps for films and TV, where it has one, so they never ring a real person.
- Random numbers, UUIDs and hex IDs.
- Give any menu item a keyboard shortcut that works in every app.
- Get Fake Text in Shortcuts, and in Spotlight on macOS 26.
- Recently used items sit at the top of the menu.
- Opens at login if you want it to.
- The menu and Settings are in English, German, French, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

Nothing is locked. Settings has three tips if you'd like to support the app.

Needs macOS 15 or later.
