This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in [Little Faker](/littlefaker).

## Little Faker Collects No Personal Information

I do not collect, use, save, or have access to any of your personal data. Little Faker has no account, no server and no network access of its own. Your settings are not personal and are stored only on your Mac.

## Tips

Settings has three optional tips. They are in-app purchases through the App Store: Apple handles the payment, and I never see your name or payment details. Little Faker remembers only whether you've tipped, on your Mac. A tip unlocks nothing.
